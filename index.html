More ways to cut wastage further
Tiered markdown, on autopilotThis board already tags 15% off at 14 days, 30% at 7, and 50%+ at 3 — so pricing staff act on the pill, not on memory.
Face out by days-left, not delivery dateWhen restocking a shelf, pull the lowest "days left" number to the front — FIFO by expiry beats FIFO by arrival when a slower-selling batch has a shorter date.
Right-size the next orderIf the same product keeps showing up in "discarded," that's a signal to order less of it next delivery, not to discount it faster.
Review the 30-day ratio weeklyA falling "cleared vs. wasted" ratio usually means a delivery is arriving with too little shelf life left — worth a note back to that supplier.
